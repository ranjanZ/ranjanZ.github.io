Ranjan Mondal

Industry Professor & Senior AI Researcher

I am an AI researcher and practitioner with a PhD in Machine Learning and over five years of industry experience at JP Morgan Chase and Samsung Research. My work bridges the gap between theoretical foundations and real-world applications, focusing on Large Language Models (LLMs), Graph Neural Networks, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Computer Vision.

Currently, I lead initiatives in scalable model serving, agentic AI systems, and financial optimization, holding multiple patents and publishing in top-tier venues like CVPR, BMVC, and IEEE TIP.

Research Areas

Large Language Models

Fine-tuning SLMs/LLMs (Llama, BERT), RLHF, DPO, and efficient serving with vLLM and Ray Serve for financial domains.

Graph Machine Learning

Scalable GNNs for peer identification, anomaly detection in company graphs, and knowledge graph construction for RAG.

RAG & Search Systems

Building end-to-end Retrieval-as-a-Service platforms, hybrid retrieval (BM25 + Dense), cross-encoder reranking, and agentic workflows.

Quantitative Trading

Algorithmic trading strategies, backtesting frameworks for NIFTY 500, and event-driven execution using agentic AI.

Computer Vision

Video panoptic segmentation, morphological networks, image dehazing/deraining, and real-time instance tracking.

Reinforcement Learning

Applying RL for decision making in trading agents and optimizing LLM alignment through PPO and DPO.
