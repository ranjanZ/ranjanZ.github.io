I am a Senior Associate in Applied AI/ML at JP Morgan Chase, where I lead initiatives in large-scale graph learning, LLM orchestration, and financial optimization. With a PhD in Machine Learning from the Indian Statistical Institute and over five years of industry experience, I specialize in bridging cutting-edge research with scalable business solutions.
