Ranjan Mondal

With five years of industry experience and a PhD in Machine Learning, I specialize in Deep Learning, Graph ML, and classical Machine Learning, applying mathematical foundations to solve complex business challenges. I have a proven track record of designing scalable ML solutions and delivering fast, business-driven implementations, including end-to-end ownership to productionize a scalable retrieval platform (Retrieval-as-a-Service) serving multiple high-impact business use cases.

My technical expertise spans SLM and LLM fine-tuning, retrieval, search and ranking systems, cross-encoder fine-tuning, RAG pipelines, and scalable model serving with Ray Serve and vLLM. I also have hands-on experience with Reinforcement Learning, Agentic RAG and Graph ML enabling me to bridge cutting-edge research with real-world AI applications.

Key Projects

  • Retrieval Platform
    Modular, Configuration-Driven Retrieval-as-a-Service Platform

    Designed and built a unified retrieval platform supporting heterogeneous structured data sources (CSV, S3, JSON). Incorporates diverse retriever suite (BM25, SPLADE, dense retrieval) with pluggable rerankers and multi-level caching. Integrated NER and entity resolution for Graph RAG with auto-evaluation framework.

  • Agentic RAG Systems
    Legal Contract & Trust-based RAG Systems

    Engineered scalable agentic RAG system with autonomous multi-step reasoning over legal contracts using Llama 3.2, achieving 2-4s latency. Built abstention-aware RAG system with hybrid retrieval and confidence-based decision layer, evaluated on SQuAD v2 and custom MBZUAI dataset (MRR 0.68-0.99).

  • Financial AI Systems
    NIFTY 500 Swing Trading Platform & Agentic News Analysis

    Developed automated backtesting platform for swing trading strategies on NIFTY 500 stocks with real-time market scanning. Built agentic framework for real-time financial news analysis categorizing M&A, investments, and product launches with dynamic tool-calling for immediate impact assessment.

  • Graph ML & Anomaly Detection
    Company Peer Identification & Anomaly Detection

    Built scalable GNN system processing 400M+ multi-modal company records using custom distance metrics and EMR. Implemented eigen centrality-based anomaly detection for company graphs handling missing data robustly.

  • LLM Fine-tuning
    LLaMA-2 (7B) Fine-tuning & BERT NER Model

    Fine-tuned LLaMA-2 (7B parameters) using 4-GPU parallelized training for structured industry summarization. Fine-tuned BERT-base NER model on domain-specific Offers data achieving high precision and recall for custom entity types.
